# Customer Case Study: AeroPro Defense ISR

**Customer:** AeroPro Defense Systems  
**Fleet:** 12x Medium Altitude Long Endurance (MALE) ISR UAVs  
**Powertrain:** Rotax 914 Turbocharged Aero-Piston Engines  
**Deployment:** AeroTwin Fleet Cloud + Edge Advisory Runtime  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

AeroPro Defense operates a fleet of 12 tactical ISR drones conducting 14-hour maritime border patrol missions. Prior to deploying AeroTwin, the operator experienced an unscheduled engine removal rate of 11.2% per year, primarily driven by unverified thermocouple spikes and uncoordinated sensor drift that triggered emergency mission aborts.

Within 90 days of deploying the AeroTwin Digital Twin, AeroPro achieved:
- **45% Reduction in Unscheduled Maintenance Downtime.**
- **Zero Premature Engine Teardowns** caused by faulty sensors.
- **$320,000 Net Annual Savings** across the 12-aircraft fleet.

---

## 2. The Operational Challenge

1. **False Sensor Aborts:** In-flight thermocouple drift on Cylinder 3 caused high-CHT cockpit alarms, forcing pilots to abort 6 mission sorties over 12 months.
2. **Maintenance Bottlenecks:** MRO teams spent an average of 42 labor hours per incident tearing down cylinders to inspect for mechanical overheating that was not physically present.
3. **Lack of Life Visibility:** Overhaul schedules were fixed strictly at 1,000 flight hours regardless of whether the aircraft flew gentle cruise or harsh desert climb profiles.

---

## 3. The AeroTwin Solution

- **Edge Physics Firewall:** Filtered transient sensor spikes using thermodynamic energy balance invariants.
- **Physics/AI Cross-Validation:** Automatically discriminated Case B (true cooling fault) from Case C (thermocouple wiring degradation), preventing false mission aborts.
- **Prescriptive Work Orders:** Provided ATA 75-00 maintenance instructions pinpointing loose baffling rather than requiring full engine replacement.
- **Executive Value Story Reporting:** Enabled automated generation of post-flight airworthiness packs for defense contract compliance.
