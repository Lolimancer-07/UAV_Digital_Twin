# UAV Digital Twin Platform: Customer Data-Use Policy & Learning Rights

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-10-09  
**Status:** Approved for Commercial & Defense Evaluation  

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This Data-Use Policy defines the legal and technical boundaries governing data ingestion, storage, telemetry isolation, and collective model improvement across the UAV Digital Twin platform. It applies to all tenants, aircraft operators, and enterprise customers utilizing Ground Control Station (GCS) and onboard edge runtime instances.

---

## 2. Fundamental Commitments

1. **Zero Raw Telemetry Pooling:**  
   Customer time-series telemetry (RPM, CHT, EGT, oil pressure, vibration, fuel flow, flight trajectories, and mission GPS coordinates) remains strictly isolated within the customer's dedicated tenant partition. Raw flight data is **never pooled, shared, or cross-referenced** across tenants.

2. **Customer Data Ownership:**  
   The customer retains exclusive ownership and intellectual property rights over all raw sensor telemetry, mission flight plans, maintenance logs, and operator decision trails generated by their fleet.

3. **Per-Tenant Opt-In for Collective Intelligence:**  
   Participation in federated model improvements is **strictly opt-in** (`opt_in_aggregate_learning = true`). Tenants who do not opt in are completely excluded from federated aggregation rounds; their local model updates are never solicited or received by the fleet coordinator.

4. **Cryptographic & Differential Privacy Guarantees:**  
   When a tenant opts into federated learning:
   - Only local parameter gradients ($\Delta w$) are transmitted—**zero raw sensor readings leave the tenant edge node**.
   - All parameter updates are bounded by $L_2$-norm gradient clipping ($\|\Delta w\|_2 \le C$).
   - Aggregation applies $(\epsilon, \delta)$-Differential Privacy via calibrated Gaussian noise injection before producing global weights.
   - The coordinator cannot reconstruct individual flights or operational profiles from clipped, privatized updates.

| **Tier 1: Confidential Flight Data** | Raw sensor telemetry, GPS coordinates, RPM/CHT, fault logs | Isolated Tenant EventStore partition (SQLite/Postgres) | Configurable by customer (default 90 days) | **STRICTLY PROHIBITED (Hard isolation enforced by RBAC & DB partition keys)** |

| **Tier 4: Global Model Artifacts** | Signed ONNX model weights (`uav_rul_model.onnx`) | Shared Model Registry | Permanent versioned history | **Available to all subscribed fleet nodes** |

## 4. Federated Learning & Differential Privacy Controls

### 4.1 Participant Attestation
Each participating edge node must present a valid cryptographic attestation (HMAC-SHA256 signature signed by the tenant's registered key). Unattested or malformed gradient submissions are rejected at the coordinator boundary.

### 4.2 Aggregation Quorum
To prevent differential reconstruction attacks, federated aggregation rounds will only execute when a minimum quorum of **at least 3 independent participants** submit attested gradients ($K \ge 3$). If quorum is not met, the round is aborted.

### 4.3 Privacy Budget ($\epsilon, \delta$)
Every federated round calculates and logs the cumulative privacy expenditure. The default enterprise configuration guarantees:
- Gradient Clipping Threshold: $C = 0.15$
- Target Differential Privacy: $\epsilon \le 4.0$, $\delta \le 10^{-5}$ per training epoch
- Noise Multiplier: $\sigma \ge \frac{\sqrt{2 \ln(1.25/\delta)}}{\epsilon}$

## 5. Drift Governance & Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards

1. **No Silent Model Mutations:**  
   When statistical distribution drift (KS-test or Population Stability Index exceedance) is detected, the platform **never modifies model weights or decision thresholds automatically**.
2. **Review Ticket Generation:**  
   Drift conditions automatically generate a formal `DriftReviewTicket` requiring review and acknowledgement by an authorized flight reliability engineer.
3. **Model Promotion Gates:**  
   Any model retraining or update triggered by drift evidence must pass offline metric gates ($R^2$, $MAE$), undergo shadow-mode evaluation against the active champion model, and record human sign-off before deployment.

---

## 6. Auditability & Export Rights

Customers maintain the right to:
1. Export complete decision envelopes and telemetry event logs in standard format (`JSONL`).
2. Verify cryptographic model manifests and signatures before applying Over-The-Air (OTA) updates.
3. Revoke federated learning opt-in at any time with immediate technical effect.
