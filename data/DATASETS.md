## 2. Provenance Definitions

- **`real`**: Recorded on physical instrumented aircraft or engine dynamometer test bench operating under real atmospheric and mechanical conditions.
- **`synthetic`**: Generated by numerical simulation, dynamic models, or hardware-in-the-loop test benches. Must declare generator version, physical model assumptions, and pseudo-random seed.
- **`public-benchmark (other domain)`**: Public dataset from an external engineering domain (e.g., turbofan, wind turbine, industrial bearing). Used strictly for algorithmic verification; must never be claimed as target-domain data.
- **`derived-benchmark (legacy)`**: Transformed or re-indexed benchmark data maintained for historical test suite stability.

---

## 3. Policy & Enforcement

1. No model may be registered in `packages/contracts/model_manifest.json` or `backend/model_card.json` without an explicit `dataset_provenance` tag.
2. Production claims, pitch decks, and README files must explicitly cite whether reported numbers derive from `synthetic` simulations or `real` engine telemetry.
3. Automated test `tests/test_model_governance_cards.py` verifies that all loaded model artifacts reference labeled datasets.
